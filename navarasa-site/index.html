What we do

Navarasa takes its name from the nine rasas of Indian aesthetic theory — every rehearsal is really about finding one of them in your body.

Workshops

Monthly sessions across different styles. Come curious — no prior dance training required, ever.

Classes

External teachers lead regular sessions in Kathak, Bollywood and Odissi — see the styles below.

Performances

Showcases, Spotlight nights and campus performances — the stage is where the work pays off.

Competitions

We compete at IICM, Karavaan and Nupura — and train hard in the weeks before each one.

Learn the form

Three styles run through the year, each led by an outside teacher who trains us properly, not just for a showcase.

CLASSICAL

Kathak

Storytelling through footwork, spins and expression.

COMMERCIAL

Bollywood

High energy, group choreography, our most-danced style.

CLASSICAL

Odissi

Sculptural poses and rhythm rooted in Odisha's temples.
