About Navarasa

Navarasa is the dance club of IISER Pune — started as a place for students to move, whatever their background or experience. We take our name from the nine rasas of Indian aesthetic theory, because that's really what a rehearsal is about: finding one of those nine emotions in your body and putting it on stage.

No auditions, no experience required — just a willingness to show up, learn, and put in the work before the next stage.
